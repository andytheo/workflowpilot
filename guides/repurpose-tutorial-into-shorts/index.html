Content workflow

Turn one useful tutorial into three short-form videos

A simple content repurposing workflow for creators who want consistent short videos.

This is an editorial process you can follow with free recording and editing tools.

Instead of making three unrelated videos, start with one helpful tutorial and identify three specific questions it answers.

Write the source tutorial

Choose a concrete problem and document a reproducible solution. Capture your own screen recordings or licensed visuals.

Make three angles

  1. The mistake: show a common error and its consequence.
  2. The quick win: demonstrate one actionable step.
  3. The comparison: explain when to choose one approach over another.

Use a consistent structure

Open with a clear problem, demonstrate one idea, mention an important limitation, and point viewers to the complete tutorial.

Publish thoughtfully

Adapt captions and safe areas to each platform. Don't assume a clip that works on YouTube will perform the same on Instagram or TikTok. Check platform rules before automating uploads.

Measure useful signals

Track clicks to the full guide, meaningful engagement and conversions. View count alone doesn't tell you whether the content is helping buyers.

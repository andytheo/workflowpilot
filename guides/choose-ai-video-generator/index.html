How to choose an AI video generator without wasting money

A practical checklist for evaluating AI video software before buying a plan.

This is a feature evaluation framework, not a hands-on ranking or endorsement of any particular vendor.

AI video generators can speed up production, but a polished demo doesn't always represent the editing time required for your own content.

Start with the output

Decide whether you need vertical shorts, tutorial screen recordings, presenter videos or captioned clips. A tool built for one format may struggle with another.

Compare these five things

  1. Export rules: check resolution, watermarks and commercial use terms.
  2. Editing control: can you replace an inaccurate clip or correct captions?
  3. Voice and captions: listen for pronunciation errors and timing.
  4. Usage limits: compare export minutes, credits and regeneration costs.
  5. Privacy: avoid uploading client footage or confidential documents without authorization.

Run the same test

Give each candidate tool the same 45-second script. Measure the time to produce a usable video, including corrections. Evaluate the final result on a phone with sound muted.

Before paying

Read the current vendor pricing page, cancellation terms and license. If a free tier is available, use it to test your workflow rather than relying only on promotional examples.

# First Short: Stop losing client inquiries

**Format:** 45–60 seconds, screen recording and voiceover.

**Hook (0–5s):** "Still copying every new client inquiry into a spreadsheet by hand?"

**Problem (5–15s):** "When messages arrive in different places, it's easy to miss a follow-up."

**Demonstration (15–45s):** "Create a simple inquiry form. Send each response into a spreadsheet. Add columns for status, owner and next follow-up date. Test the form yourself before sharing it."

**Tradeoff (45–52s):** "This is a basic tracker, not a full CRM. Check who can access customer data."

**CTA (52–60s):** "The step-by-step checklist is on WorkflowPilot."

**Caption:** "A simple form and spreadsheet can make follow-ups easier. Test the workflow before using it with real client data. Full guide: link in bio."

**Production notes:** Show a genuine test workflow. Do not claim tools or features were tested unless they were. Add affiliate disclosure when applicable.
